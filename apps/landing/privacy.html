Privacy Policy

NGMI is an Agentic Company operating on Solana. This Policy explains what we collect when you use the Interface and related services, and what we cannot collect or delete because it lives on a public chain.

Introduction

This website-hosted user interface (this “Interface”) is made available by NGMI, an Agentic Company operating on Solana (the “Company,” “us,” “we,” or “our”). NGMI is not a registered corporation. Operations, including this Interface, the keeper that settles pots, and related bots, may be performed by software agents acting for NGMI.

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) governs how we make the Interface available, and how we collect, use, maintain, and disclose information collected from users (each, a “user,” “you,” or “your”) through NGMI websites, including the Interface, web applications, associated landing pages, Telegram bots, and all sites we link from the Interface (collectively, the “Site”). This Policy also applies to information we collect in connection with our products, content, features, and related services (collectively with the Site, the “Services”).

The Services include binary USDC prediction pots on Solana. Settlement uses a public FomoScan leaderboard print posted on-chain by a dedicated resolver. We do not custody your keys. Connecting a wallet is optional for browsing and required to transact.

Information Collection

When you use the Services, we may collect the following information:

  • Wallet address. We may collect the Solana wallet address you connect to the Interface, primarily to block wallets associated with certain legally prohibited conduct, to show your positions, and as part of Usage Information below. A wallet address is a public blockchain identifier, not a legal name.
  • Device information. Device type, operating system, browser type, language, and viewport size, so we can render the Interface and debug failures.
  • Usage information. How you use the Interface: time of access, pages and pots you view, features you use, links you click, search queries, and the wallet address in session. Server and hosting logs (IP address, user-agent, referrer, request path, timestamps) may be collected by us or by the host.
  • Local storage. The Interface may store wallet connection preference and UI state in your browser (cookies or local storage). We do not use that storage to run third-party advertising.
  • Telegram. If you use the public-group feedback Menu, Telegram provides us your Telegram user id, username or display name, chat id, and the text you submit. That channel is for product feedback. Exploitable bugs go through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, not Telegram.
  • Voluntary information. If you send unsolicited information (for example a GitHub issue or an X reply), you are responsible for its accuracy. Do not send seed phrases, private keys, or secrets.

We do not ask for government ID, email, or legal name to use the Interface. We cannot see your wallet seed or private keys.

Public Solana ledger

Solana is a public blockchain. Creating a pot, placing a bet, claiming, and related program instructions are broadcast to the network. Wallet addresses, amounts, pot ids, and transaction signatures are visible to anyone with an explorer or RPC node, including us, and remain public for the life of the chain. We do not control Solana validators or indexers. We cannot amend or erase on-chain records on request.

RPC providers you or the Interface use will see the queries and transactions you send through them. Those providers have their own policies.

Use of Information

We collect and use information for these business purposes:

  • Operating the Services. Connecting your wallet to the Interface, showing pots and positions, routing transactions you sign, responding to support and feedback, sending technical notices and policy updates, and detecting fraud, Terms breaches, or harm.
  • Settlement and product. The keeper reads the FomoScan all-time leaderboard and a resolver key posts end_pnl on-chain. FomoScan data is public leaderboard data, not your private account. Operator posts (for example pot open / YES / NO on X) may include a followed FomoScan handle and tickers in that trader’s verified Solana wallet.
  • Improving the Services. Understanding how the Interface is used, fixing bugs, and other legitimate purposes permitted by law.
  • Security and law. Maintaining integrity of the Interface, investigating abuse, enforcing Terms, and complying with law, including responding to valid legal process.
  • Transfer of operations. If NGMI’s operations, agents, or assets are transferred, merged, or reorganized, information we hold may be used in connection with that transfer.
  • Consent. Where law requires consent, for the purpose you consented to.

We may use information that does not identify you (including aggregated or de-identified information) for any purpose except as prohibited by law.

Autonomous agents acting for NGMI may process the same categories of information, on the same purposes, as human operators would.

Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information as described in this Policy, including with:

  • Infrastructure. Hosting, CDN, DNS, and similar providers that run the Site.
  • Chain and data vendors. RPC nodes, explorers, FomoScan (leaderboard), and other infrastructure needed to read or submit Solana transactions.
  • Messaging platforms. Telegram (Bot API) for group feedback; X if we publish pot notices.
  • Professional advisors. Auditors, counsel, and consultants for audits or legal obligations.
  • Authorities. When we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or to protect users, the Services, or others.

Service providers who receive information from us are restricted to using it to perform services for NGMI. We do not sell personal information.

We may share information that does not identify you (including aggregated or de-identified information) except as prohibited by law. On-chain activity is already public and is not “shared” by us in the ordinary sense; anyone can read the ledger.

Third-Party Services

The Services may include links and integrations we do not operate: wallet software (Phantom, Solflare, and others), Solana RPC, FomoScan, Telegram, X, GitHub, block explorers, and other sites. When you leave the Interface or use those tools, those parties collect information under their own policies. Review them before connecting a wallet or signing.

Your use of a third-party wallet is governed by that wallet’s terms and privacy policy. We never receive your seed phrase.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect off-chain information we control from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. Transmission over the internet and public-chain broadcast are not fully secure. We cannot guarantee complete security, and we cannot protect information once it is on Solana.

Data Retention

We keep off-chain information we control only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy and as law allows. Server logs are typically retained for a limited operational window. Telegram feedback we receive may be retained as a product record.

If you ask us to delete off-chain information we control, we may still retain what law, tax, security, or dispute-handling requires. We cannot delete Solana transactions, program accounts, or copies held by third-party indexers.

Children under 18

The Site is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have, we will delete what we control unless law requires otherwise.

Transfer of Data

Off-chain information may be processed on computers outside your country, including by hosting providers and by agents acting for NGMI. Data-protection rules there may differ from yours. Using the Services means you understand that transfer. Courts or authorities in those places may be able to access information held there.

On-chain data is replicated globally by Solana validators and is not localized to your country of residence.

Your choices

  • Disconnect your wallet and stop using the Interface.
  • Clear cookies and local storage in your browser.
  • Do not use the Telegram feedback Menu if you do not want Telegram to pass us your handle and message.
  • Ask us to delete off-chain records we control (we will say what we cannot delete because it is on-chain or held by a third party).

Changes

We may revise this Policy at any time. Changes take effect when the revised Policy is posted on the Site. Continued use of the Services after posting is acceptance of the updated Policy. The effective date at the top will change when we post a revision.
