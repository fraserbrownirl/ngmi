How the pot works

The Interface is a way to view pots and to build transactions you sign with your own wallet. The program holds SPL USDC in a market vault. It does not call FomoScan. A trusted resolver posts end_pnl_usd. Default settle is first-print: the next board print over the mark may resolve YES. Close-at-T waits for the deadline print. Off-board at T, or an empty opposing pool, cancels (refunds along the program’s rules). YES if and only if end_pnl >= threshold.

A compromised or mistaken resolver can post a false print and pick the winning side. That is a stated trust assumption, not a hidden custody relationship. Published program source and audit notes are on GitHub; they do not make us your advisor or insurer.

Protocol rake, if any, is snapshotted on the market at create and paid on-chain per the program. Interface access is not a sale of the underlying USDC pot.
